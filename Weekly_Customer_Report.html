How each customer's mood is assessed each week · qualitative, based on the week's communications

  • Open issues affecting the relationship
  • Worried tone in recent communications
Watch

  • New relationship still forming
  • Quiet period, low activity
  • Routine coordination only
  • Too early to read mood
Healthy

  • Voiced positive feedback or praise
  • Steady cadence, no complaints
  • Interest in expanding scope
  • Strong overall relationship signal
