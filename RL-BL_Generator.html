Configure Run

"Auto-trace" attempts to follow the orange bore between your two clicks. Use "Straight line" for short segments or when auto-trace fails. "Tick side" decides which side of the bore the tick marks land on, based on the direction the bore travels (start → end).

Quick Help

1. Load CD — drop a Tillman CD PDF into the upload zone.

2. Pick page — typically Sheet 05 (page 7).

3. Draw mode — click along the bore to drop guide points (start at the first station, follow the curve, end at the last station). Use as many points as needed for curves like cul-de-sacs. Press Enter when done; Esc cancels.

4. Configure — enter station numbers (e.g. 0+00 → 1+00), depth, EOP. Tool generates 10-ft tick array.

5. Edit — switch to Edit tool. Click a run to select it. Drag the body to move it. Drag the big green ↻ handle (next to the blue pivot) to rotate the whole run, or drag a small green ↻ handle at the end of any tick to rotate just that one station. To delete a tick, right-click its small green ↻ handle (or its anchor dot) for the "Delete tick" menu — or click the anchor and press Delete. Shift+drag a tick anchor to nudge it sideways. Hold Shift while rotating to snap to 15° increments.

6. Export — saves PDF with vector overlay (editable in Bluebeam).

Shortcuts: D draw · E edit · M move all · Space pan · Enter finish · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ Y redo · +/− zoom · Esc cancel
